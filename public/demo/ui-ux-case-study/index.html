The Problem

New users leave before they reach value.

Onboarding asked for too much, too early. The first meaningful action was buried behind five screens, and most people never got there.

38%drop-off before step 3
4.2 minaverage time to first value
2.9/5onboarding satisfaction score
Research

People did not need fewer steps — they needed a reason to continue.

12 interviews

Users could not explain what the product would do for them after sign-up.

240 survey responses

76% wanted to try the core feature before completing their profile.

Analytics review

The largest drop happened when a long form appeared without context.

Competitive audit

Best-in-class products delay setup and show value first.

Personas

Two behaviours shaped the redesign.

The Explorer

Curious, impatient, wants to see the product immediately. Skips instructions and learns by doing.

The Planner

Wants to understand the full workflow before committing. Reads every step and values clear expectations.

Shared need

Both groups wanted a visible payoff before investing time in setup.

Design Principles

Show value first. Ask for details later.

  • Replace the mandatory form with a guided, skippable checklist.
  • Let people reach the core action in under a minute.
  • Explain why each piece of information is useful.
  • Celebrate progress with small, meaningful confirmations.
Prototype

A shorter path to the first win.

Step 1 — Intent

One question: what do you want to achieve today?

Step 2 — Guided demo

A pre-filled sample lets users experience the core flow.

Step 3 — Save & personalise

Profile details are prompted only when they add value.

Step 4 — Progress

A lightweight checklist keeps setup visible but optional.

Usability Testing

We tested the prototype with 8 participants.

Each participant completed three tasks while thinking aloud. We measured task success, time on task and perceived effort.

91%task success (up from 62%)
54%faster to first value
4.6/5ease-of-use rating
Results & Next Steps

Activation

Completion
